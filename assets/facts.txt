The distance from the Moon to the Earth averages ~384,800 km.
At 100 lkm/hr, it would take about 160 days to drive from the Earth to the Moon.
The Eiffel Tower grows about ~15 cm in summer (thermal expansion) and shrinks back in winter.
UK currency is pounds sterling, backed by the state.
Digestive biscuits are a food, not a currency.
Goldfish retain learned behaviors for weeks to months.
Bananas grow above ground in hanging clusters on the world's larger herbaceous flowering plant. Its trunk is a pseudostem.
The Empire State Building is struck by lightning roughly 25 times per year.
Local gravity is effectively constant day to day.
Weather has vertical motion (convection, precipitation) as well as horizontal.
A desert is defined by low precipitation. At 9 million square kilometers, the Sahara is the largest hot desert. Antarctica is the largest desert, even though it is not hot.
